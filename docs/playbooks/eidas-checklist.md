# eIDAS checklist (trusted lists and qualification)

What a validator of the EU trust framework (Regulation (EU) No 910/2014, eIDAS) has to show about the trusted lists
and the qualification of signatures, item by item, and what shows each item with SimpleSign. It is the input of the
eIDAS playbook (`eidas-baseline.json`, generated by `tools/playbooks/eidas.py`): every item with **P** has at least one
step tagged with its id (`L-2`, `Q-3`…), which `PlaybookValidatorTests.EidasSample_CoversEveryChecklistItemARunCanShow`
checks.

The steps run against the Sandbox's list of trusted lists (`/tl/lotl.xml`) read the eIDAS way (the `eidas` profile with
the Sandbox as its source: `--profile eidas --sandbox URL`), through the library and the CLI. With the real EU LOTL the
same code reads `https://ec.europa.eu/tools/lotl/eu-lotl.xml`, checked against the signers bundled with
SimpleSign.Europe.Eidas, following the pivot LOTLs.

**How**, for each item:

- **P** — a step of the `eidas-baseline` playbook shows it;
- **U** — covered by unit tests of the library (`SimpleSign.Europe.Eidas.Tests`, `SimpleSign.XAdES.Tests`), not by a run;
- **N** — not done yet.

## Trusted lists (ETSI TS 119 612, eIDAS art. 22)

| Id | Item | How | Steps / tests |
|---|---|---|---|
| L-1 | The LOTL is checked against its operator's certificate; a LOTL signed by another key or changed gives no anchors | P | `tl-ok`, `lotl-wrong-signer`, `lotl-tampered`, `v-tl-ok`, `v-lotl-wrong-signer` |
| L-2 | Each national list is checked against the certificate the LOTL names for it | P | `tl-ok`, `tl-wrong-signer`, `tl-tampered`, `v-tl-ok`, `v-tl-wrong-signer` |
| L-3 | An expired list is refused for signing; for validation an expired national list is judged by TLNotExpired, an expired LOTL refused | P | `tl-expired`, `lotl-expired`, `v-tl-expired` |
| L-4 | The status history of a service: a signature proven to exist while the service was granted stays trusted | P | `v-withdrawn-after-use` |
| L-5 | The keys that sign the EU LOTL are carried forward through the pivot LOTLs from those of the Official Journal | U | `TrustedListSetTests.LoadAsync_LotlSignedByANewKey_*`, `EidasExtensionTests.LotlSigners_*` |
| L-6 | Service information extensions: Qualifications (criteria lists) and AdditionalServiceInformation | U | `TrustedListQualificationsTests` |

## Qualification of the signature (ETSI TS 119 615, TS 119 172-4; eIDAS art. 3, 26, 28, 29, 32, 36)

| Id | Item | How | Steps / tests |
|---|---|---|---|
| Q-1 | A qualified certificate (QcCompliance) under a granted CA/QC service, its key on a QSCD (QcSSCD): QESig; without QC statements: AdESig | P | `tl-ok`, `q-not-qualified`, `ts-qualified`, `v-tl-ok`, `v-withdrawn-after-use` |
| Q-2 | The trusted list's Qualifications extension corrects what the certificate says (QCWithQSCD for a policy set) | P | `q-qscd-by-list` |
| Q-3 | Qualified, but no QSCD: AdESig-QC | P | `q-adesig-qc`, `q-qscd-by-list` |
| Q-4 | A qualified certificate for seals (QcType eseal): QESeal | P | `q-qeseal` |
| Q-5 | A certificate under a CA/PKC service is not qualified, whatever its QC statements | P | `q-under-pkc` |
| Q-6 | A signing policy can require a qualified certificate on a QSCD before signing (CertificateNotQualified) | P | `q-policy-refuses`, `q-policy-accepts` |
| Q-7 | An indeterminate validation gives "Indeterminate QESig", a failed one "Not AdES" (TS 119 172-4) | U | `EidasQualifierTests.Qualify_NotValid_FollowsTs119172_4` |
| Q-8 | A certificate qualified under another legislation (QcCClegislation) is not EU qualified | U | `EidasQualifier` (QcCClegislation) |

## Time-stamps (eIDAS art. 41, 42; ETSI EN 319 422)

| Id | Item | How | Steps / tests |
|---|---|---|---|
| T-1 | A time-stamp from a granted TSA/QTST service is a qualified electronic time-stamp (QTSA) | P | `ts-qualified`, `v-withdrawn-after-use` |
| T-2 | A time-stamp from a TSA service that is not TSA/QTST is not qualified (TSA) | P | `ts-non-qualified` |
| T-3 | The eidas signing policy refuses a TSA outside the lists or whose certificate is revoked | P | `ts-untrusted`, `ts-revoked` |
