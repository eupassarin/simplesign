# ETSI conformance checklist

What a run of SimpleSign must show, standard by standard, to check it against the ETSI signature standards. It is the
input of the ETSI playbook (`etsi-baseline.json`, to be generated by `tools/playbooks/etsi.py`): every item with **P**
becomes at least one step tagged with its id (`PA-3`, `VA-5`…) and its standard.

Built from what SimpleSign implements ([conformance.md](../conformance.md), [coverage.md](../articles/coverage.md),
the TestBench option schema) and shaped after the ETSI conformance test suites (TS 119 124 for CAdES, TS 119 134 for
XAdES, TS 119 144 for PAdES).

**How**, for each item:

- **P** — a playbook step shows it: a signature or a validation against the Sandbox, cross-validated with EU DSS when
  DSS trusts the same Sandbox;
- **U** — covered by unit or contract tests (`EtsiEn319142ComplianceTests`, `Contracts.Tests`), not by a run;
- **N** — needs something new first (a sample, a Sandbox case, a check);
- **X** — not supported by SimpleSign: out of scope, stated in the evidence.

## 1. Baseline signatures — production (one section per format)

The same matrix applies to each format: four levels × the attributes each level requires × each signer key type.

### EN 319 142-1 (PAdES baseline)

| # | What to validate | How |
|---|---|---|

| PA-2 | B-B must not carry the CMS signing-time; /M in the signature dictionary instead | U (add P: inspect) |
| PA-3 | B-T: signature timestamp (unsigned attribute) over the signature value; message imprint correct; TSA trusted | P |
| PA-4 | B-LT: DSS dictionary with the certificates, CRLs and OCSP responses of the signer and the TSA; VRI optional | P + U |
| PA-5 | B-LTA: document timestamp (`ETSI.RFC3161`) after the DSS, covering the whole previous revision | P + U |
| PA-6 | LTA renewal: a second document timestamp over a B-LTA (archive renewal) | P (`augment` step with `renew`: `PadesAugmenter.RenewArchive`) |
| PA-7 | Incremental updates keep earlier signatures valid; appended content after a signature is reported | P (sample `appended`) |
| PA-8 | DocMDP certification P=1/2/3 and the changes it allows | P (certification options) |
| PA-9 | Optional: signature policy (signature-policy-identifier), commitment type, signer location / reason (/Reason, /Location) | P |
| PA-10 | PDF/A preserved when asked | P (`preservePdfA`) + U |

## 4. Validation — EN 319 102-1 (and the report, TS 119 102-2)

Each row is a validation that must give the right **indication / sub-indication**. Samples exist where noted;
otherwise a sign step produces the file.

| # | Case | Expected | How |
|---|---|---|---|
| VA-1 | Intact signature, trusted chain | TOTAL_PASSED | P (`valid`, `v-valid`, `v-xades`) |
| VA-2 | Signed content changed | TOTAL_FAILED / HASH_FAILURE | P (`tampered-content`, `v-changed`, `v-changed-data`) |
| VA-3 | Signature value changed | TOTAL_FAILED / SIG_CRYPTO_FAILURE | P (`tampered-cms`, `v-tampered`) |
| VA-4 | Unknown root | INDETERMINATE / NO_CERTIFICATE_CHAIN_FOUND | P (`untrusted-root`, `v-untrusted`) |
| VA-5 | Signer revoked, no POE before the revocation | INDETERMINATE / REVOKED_NO_POE | P (`revoked`) |
| VA-6 | Revoked after the signature timestamp (POE before revocation) | TOTAL_PASSED | P (B-T, then the step revokes, then validate) |
| VA-7 | CA revoked | INDETERMINATE / REVOKED_CA_NO_POE | P (`under-revoked-ca`) |
| VA-8 | Signer expired at validation, no POE | INDETERMINATE / OUT_OF_BOUNDS_NO_POE | P (`expired`) |
| VA-9 | Expired after a valid B-LTA (long-term validation) | TOTAL_PASSED | P (`sandbox-short-lived:N` signer, B-LTA, `wait`, validate); and with the signature TSA expired too (`SandboxShortLived` TSA, archived at once, past signature validation, ADR 0027), PAdES and CAdES, against B-T only → INDETERMINATE |
| VA-10 | Not yet valid at signing | NOT_YET_VALID (INDETERMINATE) | P (`not-yet-valid`) |
| VA-11 | Revocation status unknown (OCSP unknown, CRL down) | INDETERMINATE / TRY_LATER or CHAIN_GENERAL_FAILURE | P (faults) |
| VA-12 | Weak algorithm (RSA-1024, SHA-1 CA) per the crypto suite (TS 119 312 / TS 119 322) | INDETERMINATE / CRYPTO_CONSTRAINTS_FAILURE(_NO_POE) | P (`rsa-1024`, `under-sha1-ca`) |
| VA-13 | KeyUsage without digitalSignature or nonRepudiation (a certificate with no KeyUsage extension is unrestricted, RFC 5280) | INDETERMINATE / CHAIN_CONSTRAINTS_FAILURE under a policy that FAILs on it (the default policy only WARNs, as DSS's) | P (`no-signing-key-usage`, IOFE validation policy) |
| VA-14 | Signature policy required but not available | INDETERMINATE / SIGNATURE_POLICY_NOT_AVAILABLE | P (a policy OID without its document, `preset:validation:strict`; available from the signature-policy-store or an `ISignaturePolicyDocumentProvider`) |
| VA-15 | Time-stamps out of order (content time-stamp later than the signature time-stamp) | INDETERMINATE / TIMESTAMP_ORDER_FAILURE | P (`cades-sample:v-ts-order`, from a TSA whose clock is ahead) |
| VA-16 | Signing certificate reference does not match | INDETERMINATE / NO_SIGNING_CERTIFICATE_FOUND | P (`cades-sample:v-wrong-cert-ref`) |
| VA-17 | Detached data missing | INDETERMINATE / SIGNED_DATA_NOT_FOUND | P (CAdES and XAdES detached samples validated without their data) |
| VA-18 | Validation levels: basic, with timestamps, long-term data, archival data | right process per level | P (`validation.level`) |
| VA-19 | Validation policy: constraints FAIL/WARN/INFORM change the result | per constraint | P (custom policy upload) + U |
| VA-20 | Report per TS 119 102-2: XML well-formed, namespace v1.2.1, one SignatureValidationReport per signature, ValidationObjects with POE | schema-valid | P (`expect.etsiReport: valid`, checked against the TS 119 102-2 XSD embedded in SimpleSign: `ValidationReports.ValidateEtsiXml`) |
| VA-21 | Agreement with EU DSS on all of the above | DSS agrees | P (`crossValidate` on every step; `expect.dssAgrees` once DSS trusts the same Sandbox) |

**Not in EN 319 102-1 as implemented:** POE comes from the signature time-stamp, and from a later archive or document
time-stamp when the signature time-stamp's TSA is no longer trusted (ADR 0027); a chain of several renewals is not walked
down. Sub-indications not produced today include EXPIRED, OUT_OF_BOUNDS_NOT_REVOKED and POLICY_PROCESSING_ERROR. These
are known gaps and are listed in the evidence.

## 7. Out of scope (stated, not tested)

- **ASiC (EN 319 162):** not read or written.
- **JAdES (EN 319 182):** not implemented.
- **Signature creation devices and qualified status (eIDAS QSCD):** not a library concern.
- **TS 119 1x4 interoperability Plugtests:** an external event. The playbook mirrors their structure but does not
  replace them.

## Open items (N)

None: every item a run can show is a step of the ETSI playbook.
